# Matteo Longo Minnolo

I am Matteo, currently working on a research contract (Grupo M3) at the Instituto de Física de Cantabria (IFCA). I joined the institute in October 2026, working under the supervision of Dr. Belén Barreiro. I obtained my Master's degree from the Università degli Studi di Catania, Italy. My master's thesis focused on the forecast of anisotropic cosmic birefringence for future experiments using the localization approach, which recently resulted in a [preprint article on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03876). 

<br>

Currently, my work aims to unveil the complexity of radio foregrounds for the detectability of the Cosmic Microwave Background  polarization B-mode. Specifically, I am involved in the production of galactic emission component maps based on QUIJOTE-MFI data and their combination with other spatial missions, in particular Planck and WMAP. Furthermore, my research includes the production of low-frequency extragalactic source catalogs and studying their impact on future CMB experiments, such as LiteBIRD.

### Research interests
- Cosmic Microwave Background 
- Radio foregrounds and galactic emissions
- Cosmic Birefringence
