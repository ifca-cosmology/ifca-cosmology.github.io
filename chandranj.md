# Jyothis Chandran

I am Jyothis, a PhD student in Observational Cosmology at [IFCA](https://ifca.unican.es/es-es). My study is funded by a pre-doctoral contract under the "CONCEPCION ARENAL" program of the [Universidad de Cantabria](https://web.unican.es/). I have been working under the supervision of [Mathieu Remazeilles](https://ifca-cosmology.github.io/remazeillesm) and [Rita Belén Barreiro](https://ifca-cosmology.github.io/barreirorb) since February 2022. While completing my Integrated Bachelor of Science - Master of Science (BS-MS) degree at [IISER Thiruvananthapuram](https://www.iisertvm.ac.in/), majoring in Physics and minoring in Mathematics, my thesis focused on characterising and reducing residual and noise bias in Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) observations from [PLANCK](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/planck) and [WMAP](https://map.gsfc.nasa.gov/) multi-frequency data using Harmonic ILC. I also briefly worked on understanding modified gravity (Horndeski theories) using parameters like the gravitational slip.

During my ongoing PhD, my work focuses on the "component separation" problem, specifically in the context of CMB observations and other related signals, particularly using blind and semi-blind component separation methods such as NILC and its variants. We produced the current best public all-sky Compton y-parameter map (y-map) of the thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich (SZ) effect using Planck NPIPE data ([MNRAS 2023](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stad3156)). The map is public through the [Planck Legacy Archive](https://pla.esac.esa.int) and [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7940376), and is being used by other groups. We then developed a Hybrid NILC method that uses the Planck polarization maps to further reduce the Galactic dust and synchrotron contamination in the y-map, combined with selective deprojection of CIB spectral moments to reduce the CIB contamination ([JCAP 2026](https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2026/08/062)). These y-maps are also public on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18876142).

I am currently working on the non-parametric reconstruction of Galactic free-free emission, starting from NILC and its hybrid and constrained variants and moving on to GNILC with deprojection, and on convolutional neural networks for detecting extragalactic point sources in CMB maps. As a member of the LiteBIRD collaboration, in the thermal SZ group, I am working towards a forecast for the tSZ × ISW cross-correlation. My interests also include characterising and producing cleaned maps of other components like the Synchrotron, free-free, CIB, etc.; constraining cosmological parameters using CMB and thermal SZ observations; mapping relativistic Sunyaev-Zeldovich effects to improve the understanding of matter distribution in galaxy clusters; and understanding different forms of CMB spectral distortions seeded in the pre-recombination era.
