# Keerthana Ramesh

I am a PhD researcher at the Instituto de Física de Cantabria (IFCA, CSIC-UC) in Santander, Spain. My research focuses on Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) studies, with particular interest in cosmic birefringence and the study of systematic effects in CMB observations. My doctoral project aims to develop reliable methods to measure cosmic birefringence and to investigate the impact of instrumental effects and foreground contamination on CMB polarization measurements, with applications to the future LiteBIRD satellite mission.

I completed my Master's degree in Physics at the National Institute of Technology Karnataka (NITK), Surathkal, India. For my master's thesis, titled *"Computation of the Matter and CMB Power Spectra in a Two Fluid Model of the Universe using Linear Perturbation Theory"*, I studied the evolution of matter and radiation perturbations and computed the corresponding matter and CMB power spectra using numerical methods.

During my PhD, I aim to investigate cosmic birefringence using CMB polarization data, study possible sources of systematic effects that can affect its measurement, and develop and validate analysis methods for future CMB experiments. I am also interested in understanding the theoretical implications of cosmic birefringence and its connection to physics beyond the Standard Model.

### Research interests

- Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) polarization
- Cosmic birefringence
- CMB instrumental systematics and calibration
- CMB data analysis and statistical methods
