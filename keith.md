# Sung Kei Li

I'm Keith, a Croucher Postdoctoral Fellow hosted by IFCA, funded by the [Croucher Foundation](https://croucher.org.hk/en) in Hong Kong. Before joining IFCA, I spent ten years at the [University of Hong Kong](https://www.hku.hk/), where I obtained my Bachelor's, M.Phil., and Ph.D. in Physics with [Prof. Jeremy Lim](https://www.scifac.hku.hk/people/lim-jeremy-jin-leong), jointly supervised by [Dr. Jose Maria Diego](https://ifca-cosmology.github.io/diegojm) from IFCA and [Prof. Thomas Broadhurst](https://www.ikerbasque.net/en/thomas-broadhurst) from IKERBASQUE/DIPC/UPV/EHU.

<br>

My research focuses on gravitational lensing, in particular, a subclass known as extremely magnified stars – individual stars in background strongly lensed galaxies being further magnified by foreground stars temporarily. These stars are now regularly detected by space telescopes such as Hubble and James-Webb, where we explore the astrophysical applications of these lensed stars, from mapping the nature of Dark Matter to star formation physics in the distant universe, and even the detection of the first stars in the universe.

### Research interests
- Gravitational lensing
- Extremely magnified stars
- Dark Matter
- Stellar initial mass function
