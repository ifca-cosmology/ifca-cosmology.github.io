# Alejandro Camazon Pinilla 
I'm Alejandro, an astrophysicist working at the interface between observational astrophysics, cosmology, and astronomical instrumentation. I studied Physics at the [University of Valladolid](https://universityofvalladolid.uva.es) and I obtained a Master's degree in Astrophysics at the [Complutense University of Madrid](https://www.ucm.es). I earned my PhD in Astronomy at the University of Florida, USA. Following my PhD, I held a nine-month postdoctoral fellowship at the Institut d'Estudis Espacials de Catalunya ([IEEC](https://www.ieec.cat)) and the Institute of Space Sciences ([ICE-CSIC](https://www.ice.csic.es)). I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Instituto de Física de Cantabria ([IFCA](https://ifca.unican.es/es-es)).

<br>

My research covers two complementary areas: the study of luminous compact blue galaxies (LCBGs) and their ionized gas kinematics, and the development of realistic astronomical simulations for low-surface-brightness (LSB) science in the context of the ARRAKIHS and Euclid missions. 

I combine the study of galaxies with the development of tools to improve the scientific return of astronomical observations. On the observational side, I have investigated the kinematics and ionized gas properties of compact star-forming galaxies in the local and in the high redshift universe using Megara and EMIR instruments from GTC. On the instrumentation and simulation side, as Instrument Scientist of ARRAKIHS mission, I assess the performance of the instrument, developing ATREIDS (ARRAKIHS Telescope Realistic Exploration and Imaging Detection Simulations), an end-to-end simulation framework for the ARRAKIHS mission. This work involves modeling astrophysical scenes, diffuse backgrounds, straylight, and detector effects, with the aim of understanding the detectability and characterization of faint structures around galaxies.

### Research interests
- Galaxy formation and evolution
- Luminous compact blue galaxies and ionized gas kinematics
- Observational cosmology
- Low-surface-brightness astrophysics
- Astronomical image simulations and detector modelling
- Diffuse backgrounds and straylight

### Collaborations & Missions

- [ARRAKIHS](https://www.arrakihs-mission/) — Simulations and instrumental modeling for low-surface-brightness science.
- [Euclid](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Euclid) — Astronomical imaging and simulations for cosmological and extragalactic science.
- [Gran Telescopio Canarias (GTC)](https://www.gtc.iac.es/) — Integral-field spectroscopy with MEGARA.
- [Pyxel](https://esa.gitlab.io/pyxel/) — Detector simulation framework for astronomical imaging.
